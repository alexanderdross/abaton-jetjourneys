Purpose of this document

This is the implementation brief for the public Elegant Islands journey page in the ABATON website relaunch. It translates the internal operating plan into customer-facing storytelling while deliberately withholding unnecessary operational detail, supplier logic and proprietary route-planning know-how.


### 1. Role of Elegant Islands in the Relaunch

Elegant Islands should be the primary Signature Journey presented as the currently released ABATON departure for 2027. It is the journey that receives the strongest visual treatment, the clearest call to action and the most complete public itinerary.

Website status

NOW BOOKING / RESERVATIONS OPEN. Use only once the commercial release is approved. Until then use PRIVATE PREVIEW.

Public group size

6-8 guests. Keep this consistent across homepage, journey page, enquiry form and all sales material.

Primary markets

USA, Canada, Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Secondary: selected international luxury travel advisors.

Core promise

A founder-hosted private jet journey through Ireland and Scotland, combining places that are cumbersome to connect commercially.

Internal relaunch note: Do not present the broader portfolio as equally bookable. Elegant Islands should carry the strongest “released” status; future journeys can sit behind an Interest List.

CORE CUSTOMER PROPOSITION

The reason to book

Not simply “Ireland and Scotland by private jet”. The proposition is access to a sequence of iconic estates, landscapes and cities that would otherwise require long road transfers, awkward connections and repeated airport backtracking - delivered in an intimate 6-8 guest format and personally hosted by the founder.

### 7. Day-by-Day - Final Public Copy


#### DAY 1 - LONDON

The journey begins in London, where guests arrive individually and settle into Mayfair before meeting the small ABATON circle for the first time. In the evening, a relaxed welcome brings everyone together over dinner and introduces the rhythm of the days ahead.

Overnight: London · Welcome dinner included


#### DAY 2 - LONDON TO KILLARNEY

After breakfast, a private transfer leads to the business aviation terminal for the first private flight of the journey. In little over an hour, London gives way to the green landscapes of southwest Ireland. The afternoon offers a gentle introduction to Killarney National Park and its lakes before dinner overlooking the Irish countryside.

Overnight: Killarney · Private flight · Killarney National Park


#### DAY 3 - KILLARNEY & THE GAP OF DUNLOE

Today is devoted to County Kerry at its most atmospheric. A privately arranged experience combines the Gap of Dunloe, the Black Valley and the Lakes of Killarney, using traditional local transport and time on the water. The afternoon returns to a slower pace, with space for the spa, the lake or simply the view.

Overnight: Killarney · Private landscape experience


#### DAY 4 - KILLARNEY TO ASHFORD CASTLE

A short private flight replaces hours of driving across Ireland. From the west coast, a chauffeured transfer leads through the countryside to Ashford Castle. After arrival and lunch on the estate, an intimate falconry experience provides an unforgettable first encounter with this historic property.

Overnight: Ashford Castle · Private flight · Falconry


#### DAY 5 - ASHFORD CASTLE

A full day is left for the estate. Guests choose from selected country pursuits such as horseback riding or clay shooting, while those seeking a quieter pace may enjoy the spa or the grounds. Later, the group takes to Lough Corrib before dressing for an elegant evening within the castle.

Overnight: Ashford Castle · Choice of estate activity · Private lake experience


#### DAY 6 - IRELAND TO THE SCOTTISH HIGHLANDS

The journey crosses the Irish Sea by private jet and continues by chauffeur into Wester Ross. Here, the landscape becomes larger, quieter and more remote. The afternoon is deliberately unhurried, allowing time to settle into the Highlands before an informal dinner and, for those who wish, a first tasting of Scotch whisky.

Overnight: Scottish Highlands · Private flight


#### DAY 7 - THE SCOTTISH HIGHLANDS

A private Highland day is shaped around the landscape and the interests of the group - from a scenic guided exploration to walking or time on the water. The experience is intentionally personal rather than prescriptive. The evening closes with a destination dinner celebrating Scotland’s produce and sense of place.

Overnight: Scottish Highlands · Private tailored experience


#### DAY 8 - LOCH NESS TO EDINBURGH

On the way back towards Inverness, the group pauses for a private experience on Loch Ness before taking a short private flight to Edinburgh. The afternoon introduces the city’s castle and historic centre, followed by an intimate Scotch whisky experience and a final dinner together.

Overnight: Edinburgh · Loch Ness · Private flight · Farewell dinner


#### DAY 9 - EDINBURGH TO LONDON

After breakfast, a final private flight returns the group to London. Chauffeur arrangements can then connect guests with central London, Heathrow or individual onward travel.

Private flight to London · Individual onward journey

### 14. FAQ - Recommended Website Copy


**Is this a private charter for one couple or family?**

Elegant Islands is a hosted small-group journey limited to 6-8 guests. The private aircraft is shared exclusively by the ABATON group during the published flight sectors.


**Which aircraft will be used?**

ABATON selects an aircraft suitable for the route, group size and operational requirements. The final aircraft and operator are confirmed before departure and may vary from the illustrative aircraft shown.


**How much luggage can I bring?**

Private aircraft have different baggage limitations from commercial airlines. Guests receive a journey-specific baggage guide before departure; soft-sided luggage is generally recommended.


**How active is the journey?**

The itinerary balances cultural visits and outdoor experiences with free time. Selected activities can be adapted to different comfort and activity levels wherever possible.


**Can dietary requirements be accommodated?**

Yes. Booked guests can provide allergies, intolerances and preferences in the private guest area before departure. These are shared with the relevant hospitality partners.


**Can I arrive in London earlier or stay longer?**

Yes. Additional hotel nights and individual chauffeur arrangements can be requested through the ABATON concierge service.


**When is the departure confirmed?**

The booking documentation will state the conditions under which the journey is confirmed, including any applicable minimum participant requirement.


**Will every programme detail be exactly as published?**

The character and principal destinations of the journey remain as described. Individual timings, restaurants, activities or routing details may be adjusted where operational conditions or local availability require an equivalent or improved alternative.

### 16. Image & Design Direction

Elegant Islands should look editorial and cinematic rather than like a traditional escorted-tour page. Use fewer, stronger images with generous spacing and restrained typography.

HERO IMAGE

• One wide emotional landscape image - ideally Ashford Castle, Killarney lakes or dramatic Highland scenery.

• Avoid a generic private-jet cabin as the main hero. The destination is the aspiration; aviation is the enabler.

IMAGE SEQUENCE

• London: elegant architectural / Mayfair detail, not a tourist skyline collage.

• Killarney: lakes, horse-drawn carriage, mountain landscape.

• Ashford Castle: exterior, estate life, falconry.

• Highlands: wilderness, loch, road or lodge atmosphere.

• Edinburgh: historic skyline or castle at golden hour.

• Aviation: one or two understated images of aircraft/FBO/private boarding, placed between destination chapters.

AVOID

• Image galleries with twenty near-identical destination photos.

• Heavy black-and-gold “billionaire luxury” styling.

• Overuse of jet interiors, champagne glasses or staged luxury clichés.

• Crowded icon grids and discount-style urgency elements.

### 18. Information That Should Remain Internal

• Why particular regional airports were selected or the underlying airport-selection methodology.

• Jet operator names before contracting, aircraft positioning strategy, empty-leg logic or internal flight costing.

• Hotel net rates, tour-operator rates, commissions, deposits or cancellation negotiations.

• Supplier contact names, operational addresses used purely for logistics and chauffeur dispatch details.

• Exact minute-by-minute timings more than is necessary for the guest to understand the journey.

• Backup hotels, weather alternatives and operational contingency plans.

• Unreleased future routes or the criteria used to decide which journeys move from Interest List to booking.

Public detail principle

Show enough detail to make the journey tangible and trustworthy, but not so much that the website becomes the operating manual. The public page sells the story; the internal dossier runs the journey; the member portal provides confirmed guest detail.
